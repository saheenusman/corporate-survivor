# Corporate Survivor — One More Quick Thing™

A 3D workplace survival comedy. It's Monday, 8:57 AM. Survive until 5:30 PM.

**Play:** https://saheenusman.github.io/corporate-survivor/

- **Phone:** play in landscape. Drag the left side to walk, swipe the right side to look, and tap the yellow button to interact.
- **Full screen:** on Android the game goes full screen when you tap Play (Pause → Full screen toggles it).
- **Best experience:** add it to your home screen for an app with no browser bars.
  - Chrome: ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* / *Install app*
  - iPhone: Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Desktop:** WASD to walk, drag to look, E to interact, Esc to pause.

The whole game is one self-contained `index.html`: characters, office, signs and sound are all generated in code. Built with three.js.
